Good to know

Does it hurt?

No. Lymphatic drainage works just under the skin, so the pressure stays light.

When can I start after surgery?

Only once your surgeon clears you — usually a few days to two weeks, depending on the procedure.

Is it safe during pregnancy?

From the second trimester, with a normal pregnancy and your doctor's agreement.

How many sessions do I need?

For general puffiness, many clients come once or twice a month.

Where is the studio?

A private studio in Dubai — the exact address is shared once your appointment is confirmed.
