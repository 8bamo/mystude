The Story

The thinking athlete

Germany, late 1950s. Guided by his principle of the “thinking athlete”, coach Karl Adam develops the training plans together with his rowing crew — a revolutionary approach meant to carry the German Eight to the 1960 Olympic Games in Rome.

Between punishing training sessions, personal rivalries and the shadows of Germany's past, the crew is fighting for more than a medal — for a new idea of sport and responsibility.

At the heart of it is Hans “Ari” Lenk, torn between his passion for rowing and his reckoning with the Nazi past of the world around him.
